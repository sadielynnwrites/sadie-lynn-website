A LETTER FROM SADIE LYNN

Between the Pages

Stories, secrets, and the occasional spectacular distraction.

There's more to every story than what's written on the page.

Come for the books. Stay for the characters who refuse to leave our heads, the stories behind the stories, and the occasionally chaotic process of bringing it all to life.

Expect book news, exclusive reader goodies, behind-the-scenes moments, editing adventures with my fellow author and best friend, Amanda Nichole (Vegas Wolf Pack Series), and the occasional dispatch from a brain currently chasing approximately 900 squirrels.

No two letters are quite alike. That's half the fun.

Pull up a chair.

There's always room for another story.
